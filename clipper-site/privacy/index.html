Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Clipper Studio is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It cuts videos into short clips and publishes them to the owner's own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram) when the owner requests it.

Data we access

When you connect an account we receive your basic profile information (display name, avatar, account ID) and access tokens that allow uploading videos on your behalf, using only the permissions you approve.

How data is used

Data is used only to show which account is connected and to publish the videos you choose, at the time you choose. We do not sell, share or transfer your data to any third party.

Storage

All tokens, settings and videos are stored locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is stored on external servers.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect any account at any time from the Accounts page, which deletes its tokens. You can also revoke access from your TikTok, Google or Instagram account settings.

Contact

For any question, contact the app owner.
